Market Leaders

The competitive landscape we navigate.

The prediction market space is dominated by two clear leaders. SIGNIFYTICS fuses the best of both worlds — decentralised innovation and regulatory rigour.

Polymarket

Web3 Pioneer
  • Positioning: Decentralised, permissionless, crypto-native
  • Tech: Built on Polygon blockchain, on-chain transparent data
  • Valuation: ~$9B+ | Cumulative volume: $560.7B
  • Strength: High liquidity, low slippage, global reach, user privacy

Kalshi

Compliance Trailblazer
  • Positioning: Regulated, fiat-friendly, CFTC-licensed
  • Tech: Centralised DB under strict CFTC regulation and audits
  • Valuation: ~$20B–$22B | US market share: ~89%
  • Strength: Regulatory moat, institutional trust, fiat on/off-ramps
  • Users: Monthly active users: 5.1M+
