Emergency Medical Bills — Approved in 48 Hours

Situation

Unexpected medical bills totaling $7,500 with no emergency savings available

Action

Sweet Cash evaluated income, credit, and repayment capacity, then referred to a payroll-deducted lender

Outcome

Loan approved within 48 hours; structured repayment through employer payroll
